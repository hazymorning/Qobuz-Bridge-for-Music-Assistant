    3. Speakers in the Qobuz app

    When on, new players are added automatically and players hidden in Music Assistant are left out. Switch it off to pick players yourself.

      4. Party speakers

      A party speaker is one more speaker in the Qobuz app. Its music plays on a main speaker, and the speakers you pick join that speaker while the party plays.
